Ticket

Matcha's end-of-book screen as a plugin: the finished book as a ticket torn in
two, the cover and title on one half with a 読了 stamp (读完, 讀完 or 睇完 for
Chinese and Cantonese books) and the start and finish dates, the reading time
and days on the stub, your Nth book overall and in its language. Below it: Home,
and Continue with the next book in the folder.

It opens when you leave a book you read to the end. Back returns to the book.
Open it from Plugins to see the ticket of the last book you finished.

The numbers come from the Insights plugin, so install that too. Text is drawn
with matcha's own UI fonts (in fonts/), so it turns with the ticket as in
matcha. Lookups and saved sentences are not shown: CrossPoint does not record
them. Needs a
CrossPoint build with Dessen app-event handlers (feat/dessen-apps, 20 Nov 2026
or later) on a board with PSRAM.
